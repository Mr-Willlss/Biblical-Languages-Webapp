Privacy Policy

Biblical Languages keeps learning data private and purpose-driven.

This policy explains what data the Biblical Languages platform collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The app is designed to help learners study Greek and Hebrew through lessons, progress tracking, and account sync.

What we collect

Account details such as your email address, display name, and optional profile information; lesson progress and settings; and limited technical data needed to keep the app working.

How we use it

To sign you in, sync your study progress, show achievements, remember preferences, support community features, and keep the service secure.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data, run third-party ad tracking in the app, or ask for more information than we need for learning and account features.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026

Biblical Languages is a learning platform for Koine Greek and Biblical Hebrew. We use Firebase-based authentication and database tools to support sign-in, profile sync, lesson progress, settings, and related app features.

Information we may collect

  • Account information such as email address, password hash handled by the authentication provider, display name, and profile photo if you use one.
  • Learning data such as completed lessons, scores, XP, streaks, settings, and saved preferences.
  • Optional profile content you choose to add, including username, bio, study goal, language choice, and social visibility settings.
  • Limited device and usage data needed for security, troubleshooting, and service reliability.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and keep you signed in.
  • To save your progress across devices and restore your learning state.
  • To personalize the app experience, such as your active language and lesson preferences.
  • To improve performance, diagnose errors, and maintain platform security.

Sharing and third parties

  • We may share data with service providers that support the app, such as Google/Firebase, to operate authentication and cloud storage.
  • If you sign in with Google, Google processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or brokers.

Retention and control

  • We keep account and learning data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service.
  • You can update your profile and preferences inside the app.
  • To request deletion or access help, contact us using the email below.

Children and families

Biblical Languages is an educational app and may be used by learners of different ages. If a local law requires parental consent for a child’s account or data collection, that consent should be obtained before use.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data. No system is completely secure, but we work to limit access to the information needed to run the platform.
